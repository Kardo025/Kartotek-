Plan an end-of-lesson test for the lesson below. The test measures, objective by objective, what the learner fully learned in this lesson; each objective becomes a review card.

Sources:
{{manifest}}

Split the lesson into parts that follow its own order, with 2–6 objectives per part. One objective is one testable idea: a fact, a concept or a procedure; split objectives that combine several ideas. For each objective give:
- "title" (a few words) and "kind": "fact", "concept" or "problem".
- "objective": one sentence that starts "Without notes, …" and ends with an observable performance ("… calculate …", "… explain why …").
- "refs": where it is in the sources, for example "slide 4", "p. 2" or "transcript 12:40".
Each part has a "title" and "refs". Leave out course administration. In "not_covered" list things the sources only name without explaining. Give the test a "title".
