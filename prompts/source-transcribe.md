Write in "text" everything that is written on the page, in reading order: headings, sentences, bullet points, labels, equations (in plain text such as "v = d / t" or "x^2"), table rows (cells separated by " | "). Keep the source's own words and symbols; do not explain, correct or add anything.

In "figures" describe each diagram, chart or photo in one or two factual sentences: what is drawn and what its labels say. Do not interpret beyond what is visible.

In "unreadable" list every region you could not read with certainty (for example "the exponent in the second equation"). Never guess a word, number or sign you cannot see clearly; list it there instead.
