Step 2 of writing a Kartotek study: list the relations between the source units below that the source itself supports.

A relation connects two ideas: dependency (A needs B), causal (A causes or changes B), comparative (A differs from B in …), compositional (A is part of B), sequential (A comes before B), conditional (A holds only when …). Give for each:
- "from" and "to": short names of the two ideas, in the source's terms.
- "type" and "direction" ("forward" means from → to; "bidirectional" when it goes both ways).
- "conditions": the conditions under which it holds, as the source states them ([] if none).
- "units": the unit ids that support it.

List only relations the source states or clearly entails, typically a few per page of content. Do not invent every possible pair. If there are none, answer with an empty list.
