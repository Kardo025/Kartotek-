Solve each question below yourself, using only the source pages that follow. You do not see the intended answers; Kartotek compares your answers with them afterwards, so work independently and carefully.

For every question give one entry in "answers" with:
- "ref": the question's ref, unchanged.
- "choice": for multiple-choice questions, the option ids you select (one for a single-answer question, every correct one for a "select all" question); [] otherwise.
- "number": for numeric questions, your result as a number without its unit; "" otherwise.
- "answer": your answer in words (for written questions a complete answer of a few sentences; for others a short statement of your result).
- "confidence": "sure", "unsure", or "cannot_solve" when the source does not give what is needed.
- "reasoning": the key steps, with page or slide references.
