A unit is one substantive piece of content: a definition, proposition, relation, procedure, equation, condition, exception, example, figure, table or exercise. Course administration (contacts, schedules, grading rules, links), decoration, title pages and repeated headers are not units: leave them out.

For every unit give:
- "source": the file id (S1, S2 …) and "loc": the page or slide exactly as marked in the text, for example "p. 4" or "slide 7" (a unit spread over two pages may use "pp. 4–5").
- "type": one of the unit types above.
- "text": a paraphrase in 25 words or fewer. Never copy long passages. Keep formulas exact, with their symbols and units.
- "disposition": "included" for content the study should teach; "out_of_scope" for content outside the scope (give "reason"); "unresolved" for content that cannot be taught reliably because it is unreadable, incomplete or contradictory (give "reason"). Use "" as the reason of included units.

Also give:
- "title": a short, descriptive title for a study of this material.
- "conventions": standing assumptions the source states (for example "Angles are in radians"); [] if none.
- "gaps": things the source refers to but does not give (a missing figure, a formula without its variables), each with "what" and "why".
- "not_covered": terms or results the source only names without explaining.
